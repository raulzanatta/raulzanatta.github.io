About Me

I am a PhD student in Statistical Sciences at the University of Padova, working under the supervision of Giovanna Menardi.

My research focuses on clustering methods for high-dimensional data. I have worked on modal clustering, and my current research interests also include biclustering, coclustering, and the inferential aspects of clustering.

My work is motivated by both methodological questions and real-world applications, particularly in genomics.
